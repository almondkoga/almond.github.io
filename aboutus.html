Company history

In 1083, Dr. Kristen Wright and Saria founded Rhine Labs in an apartment near the Trimounts Institute of Technology, where they had both studied. Their first projects focused on high-energy physics and life sciences. For Kristen, the work also carried a family ambition: to explore the space beyond Terra. Muelsyse joined them a year later. In 1085, Jara Wilson helped the team secure the funding it needed to grow beyond that first office.

By 1092, Rhine Labs had a new headquarters and dedicated teams for scientific investigation and engineering. That engineering work now extends to equipment for water handling and resource extraction. Our water pump and purification unit handle transfer and treatment, while our hydro driller and electric and portable mining rigs serve drilling and extraction sites.

Our development boards give engineers a way to monitor that equipment. The M32 ESP32 DevKit lets you build a wireless sensor and send its readings over Wi-Fi. The R1 Single-Board Computer can collect and process those readings locally, giving you a starting point for a connected monitoring system.

About us / Leadership

Kristen Wright

Chief Executive Officer

Kristen Wright leads Rhine Labs and heads its Component Control section. A graduate of the Trimounts Institute of Technology, she holds a Ph.D. in particle physics and is the Wright family’s only daughter.

Her business judgment helped Rhine Labs expand rapidly. As a researcher, she looks beyond Terra’s skies to the stars. Her ability to draw other scientists into that vision has earned her the support of some of Terra’s brightest minds.

Muelsyse

Chief Operating Officer

Muelsyse was one of the first members of Rhine Labs. As COO, she oversees the company’s day-to-day operations while continuing to pursue her own goals.

Saria

Chief Financial Officer

Saria developed her resilience during difficult years at the Trimounts Institute of Technology. Those experiences shaped her ambitions as a researcher. She co-founded Rhine Labs with her close friend Kristen Wright, hoping to spare others the hardships she had faced.
